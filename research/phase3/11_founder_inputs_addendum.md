## 2. Prior probability research (weak evidence)
No dataset gives the probability that *a viable paid product exists for this idea*. I used general base rates as a starting point. All are secondary sources of low reliability; primary reports (Startup Genome, Carta) were not read.

| Base rate | Figure | Source (secondary) |
|---|---|---|
| Failed startups citing "no market need" | 35–42% (founder self-reports; editions differ) | [Entrepreneur](https://entrepreneur.com/article/238088), [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/analysis/startups-fail-founders-capital/) |
| Startups reaching $1,000 MRR / $10,000 MRR | ≈ 1 in 10 / ≈ 1 in 40 (revenue-based dataset of 8,600+ startups, relayed by a blog) | [WinSavvy summary](https://www.winsavvy.com/?p=67311) |
| Indie/bootstrapped SaaS reaching $10,000 MRR | 6.1% (3,787 tracked products; median $145/month; selection-biased) | [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-indie-saas-revenue-2026) |
These describe whole ventures, not "a viable paid product exists here", so they set a **starting range of ≈ 2.5–10%**.
**Adjustments (judgment):** up for a strong technical team and an evidence-first approach; down for zero demonstrated willingness to pay, a primary-source finding that free rentable tools cover most of the workflow, no network, and limited cash.
**Updated prior: ≈ 5–8%** (previously 8–15%). This is at or just below the break-even prior of ≈ 6.5% for the redesigned plan (range 5–21% depending on assumptions; `08` §3).

## 3. What the inputs change
- **No contacts** → Stage 1 depends on cold outreach (reply rates ≈ 0.45–3%, `03`) and on recruiting accountants from zero. The chance that Stage 1 ends "not run" is high, and reaching that conclusion would cost many hours.
- **Very limited cash** → the cost of a failed experiment includes lost income time, not only the $400 cash cap. The plan should buy as much information per hour as possible and stop early.
- **120+ hours available** → affordable in principle, but at a 5–8% prior spending all of it up front is not justified.

## 4. Updated plan: two cheap gates before any customer-facing work

| Step | Purpose | Hard cap | Pass condition | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate 0 — bake-off** (`04` §3) | Does an existing tool already explain seeded margin/cash cases acceptably? | **25 hours, ≈ $0** | No existing tool is acceptable on ≥ 2 of 3 seeded scenarios under the pre-registered definition | **Stop (D)** |
| **Gate A — access test** (new) | Can you reach anyone at all without a network? | **15 hours** over 3 weeks, **no selling, no pricing** | ≥ 5 non-selling discovery conversations with accountants, bookkeepers or Shopify/wholesale owners **and** at least one accountant or fractional CFO willing to introduce a business | **Stop (D)**, or park until a network exists |
| **Stage 1** (`04` §4) | Free scan → $750 paid investigation, sequential rule | **≤ 95 hours** (unchanged) | As in `05` | As in `08` §6 |

**Total committed now: ≤ 40 hours (Gate 0 + Gate A).** Stage 1 starts only if both gates pass **and** a warm path to at least 10 qualified businesses exists (introductions or a hand-built list with verified fit).
**Gate A guidance (non-selling):** ask about how they investigate margin and cash today, what they pay, who decides, what they would not trust software to do, and whether they use Claude, Intuit's AI or Shopify's AI. Record what was actually said; do not pitch, do not quote prices, do not request data.

## 5. Updated decision
**B, narrowed: approve Gate 0 and Gate A only (≈ 40 hours). Stage 1 is not approved until both pass.** The default on any failure is **D (stop)**. Given the updated prior (≈ 5–8%) and the founder's constraints, **stopping after Gate 0 or Gate A is a fully acceptable and likely outcome**, and counts as a successful use of the experiment.

## 6. What remains unchanged
Caps for Stage 1 (≤ 95 h; total ≤ 120 h only if reached), cash cap (≤ $400), measurement (`05`), success/failure criteria (`08` §5–6), conditions to build (`08` §7) and to abandon (`08` §8), and the evidence limits listed in `00` §3: no customer voice was obtained, most primary hosts were blocked, and all probabilities are judgment.
