## 2. Counts from the evidence auditor (48 claims)
31 **verified in a search snippet** (not a full page), 13 **partially supported** (two not supported as worded: the "3–5% revenue leak" label and "recoveries fell from $400K to under $40K"), 3 **unverifiable**, 1 mixed. **No claim was verified from a full page.** Phase 2's numbers were mostly quoted correctly; the risk was in the inferences drawn.

## 3. The ten most dangerous overstatements (summary; detail in the appendix)
1. "Behavior contradicts the premise" (pass-through surveys). 2. Sizing the prize with $100M–$1B estimates for a $10–75M ICP. 3. "Recoveries decay." 4. NetSuite Ask Oracle as a substitute. 5. Margin "frequency: High" from a 2025 expectation survey. 6. Timing/cost assumptions in the same column as platform facts. 7. "27% vs 55%" small-vs-large pricing-tool investment, which could not be found (and four unrelated 27% figures appear in the evidence set). 8. "94% offer advisory" as proof of reach. 9. "Nobody has been shown to pay" while hosts were blocked. 10. Platform-term evidence under-claimed (Xero ban, Merge price, Fathom price).

## 4. Other defects found
- **Stale sources:** NAW/MDM 62% and one-third (spring 2025); Techaisle (undated); Spider 2.0 / o1-preview and the Vals v2 benchmark (superseded); SMB Group role shares (edition unclear).
- **Internal inconsistencies:** price bands differ across files ($19–$300 vs $35–$300 vs $19–$760); DSG/Cavallo figures do not reconcile; two Phase 1 appendices disagree on Spider 2.0 (21.3% vs 5.7%; use 17–21%); the 13% and 27% survey numbers are joined across different surveys; three onboarding thresholds (≤8 h pass, 16 h base case, >12 h kill) conflict.
- **Accountant "pilots" are free** in the Phase 2 plan while the headline rule is "pay before results"; the "15 qualified prospects" denominator mixes three cohorts.
- **Platform facts that are fine as written:** Shopify's 60-day default for public apps; 0% revenue share on the first $1M lifetime (add the separate 2.9% processing fee); legacy custom-app creation closed 2026-01-01 (a Dev Dashboard route remains); Odoo API gating; Amazon 90-day settlements; Intuit tiers; Merge's published $650/month for 10 linked accounts; Fathom's $450 for 25 companies; Chaser's $259 Compact tier; Profit Pulse AI at $19; Conjura–Wayflyer (2026-06-19); Stocky end of life (2026-08-31).

## 5. What the audit does to the decision

| Decision element | Effect |
|---|---|
| Do not build yet | **Stronger** (Xero clause confirmed; free rentable AI confirmed from primary pages; margin evidence weaker than stated). |
| Run a paid audit | **Direction kept, design rejected** (see `04`). |
| Margin as lead hypothesis | **Weaker.** The impact and frequency evidence is mismatched to the ICP and sponsor-authored; the "behavior contradicts" argument is inconclusive, not negative. Margin remains on the list because every alternative has its own proof gap and margin has the broadest expansion path — a judgment, not an observation. |
