# 09 — Final Recommendation to the Founder

## The decision in one paragraph
**Don't build yet. Spend 30 days and under ~$500 selling a paid "Profit & Cash Leak Audit" to Shopify + QuickBooks businesses, delivered by hand, so that money — not research — tells you which first module deserves engineering: Margin Investigator, a cash/AR module, or neither.** This is Outcome **C** from your brief, refined into a testable plan. Margin Investigator is a live hypothesis, not a winner.

## Why I am not telling you to build Margin Investigator
You asked for a decision that survives scrutiny. The evidence I could gather says:
1. The problem is real, but **no one in any category I examined has been shown to pay for an *explanation***. Visibility sells for $19–$300/month; sync sells; recovery sells on contingency. A $349–$999/month investigation product has no named paying small-company customer anywhere in the research.
2. Margin Investigator's differentiation is **partial**: each element has a cheap or free substitute (a $19 Shopify exception queue, free PVM spreadsheets, NetSuite "why did margins drop", QuickBooks/Xero AI insights, Claude for Small Business). The remaining gap — cross-system evidence, honest uncertainty, follow-up — is *unclaimed*, which could mean opportunity or could mean nobody wants it.
3. The customer segment you can reach by inbound (Shopify brands) cannot pay the price; the one that could pay (distributors) isn't reachable by inbound and reports passing costs through.
4. No alternative is better on the evidence. The top-ranked alternative, cash/AR, wins on ease (data GREEN, hours to onboard), not on proof of demand, and it is the category Intuit and Xero are bundling.

## What I'd do this week
1. **Write down the pre-registered thresholds** from `07` (≥ 4/15 pay; ≤ 8 hours onboarding; ≥ 70% accuracy; blind preference) and your **cash floor and founder draw**. Do this before talking to anyone.
2. **Do the Week-1 desk tasks**: Census counts, keyword volumes, Xero/Intuit/Shopify terms (read the primaries), the full MDM/Baird-NAW and DSG surveys, 15–20 real Reddit/HN threads (read by hand; this session could not), and hands-on trials of NetSuite Ask Oracle, Polar, Lifetimely, Hypersonix and a Claude/QuickBooks prompt.
3. **Find one friendly Shopify + QuickBooks business** and run the audit by hand, timing every hour and recording cost coverage. That single exercise will tell you more about onboarding cost than any report.
4. **Draft the audit offer, the landing page and the free file-in/leak-list-out checker spec.** (The checker is the one place your backend strength turns into inbound — build it as a throwaway, not production code.)
5. **Make a list of 10 accountants/fractional CFOs and 5 ERP resellers/trade contacts** and send the first messages. Outreach is unavoidable for these; inbound will not deliver them.

## First 30 days
Follow `07_30_day_validation_plan.md`: Week 1 desk work and assets; Week 2 launch and recruit; Week 3 deliver paid audits and run blind comparisons; Week 4 measure, apply the rules, write the result memo.

## What happens next, by outcome
- **Margin wins (A):** build the narrow Margin Loop from `05` — two connectors, reconciliation/coverage checks, two-period bridge, supplier-cost lag detector, case file, weekly Brief, decision log — in 8–12 weeks, for the stack and ICP the test validated. Keep human onboarding capped and priced.
- **Another module wins (B):** repeat a 30-day paid test for that module at ≥ $199/month, then build it; margin remains a later expansion because it touches every other module.
- **Nothing passes (C/stop):** do not rationalize. Re-pick the market (distributor/ERP-reseller track, recovery-style wedge, or advisor-as-customer) or stop. Keep the case-file template, labeled incident log and timing data — they are reusable.

## How this connects to the Company Intelligence vision
The vision (a continuously updated understanding of a business, with investigations, decisions and outcomes) is a **destination, not a first product**. The audit builds its raw material: entity maps, a hypothesis library, a labeled incident log, an evidence format and outcome records. Margin, cash and reconciliation share one Shopify + accounting connection, so whichever wins first, the other two are natural second modules. Do not commit to the broad platform until one module has paying customers.

## What I could not verify (do not rely on)
No interviews or primary customer evidence exists; most primary pages and Reddit were blocked, so all competitor, pricing and statistics evidence comes from search-result summaries (see `10_source_register.md` and each appendix file's limits section). The scores are my judgment and unstable under reasonable reweighting. Several claims need primary confirmation before use: Xero's AI-training clause, Shopify/Intuit/Xero API terms and limits, NetSuite Ask Oracle's actual output, Baird-NAW/DSG pass-through data, and all Census/Shopify counts.

## What would make me wrong
- If ≥ 4 of 15 qualified prospects **pay** for margin findings and prefer the case file to their in-product AI, this memo was too cautious: build Margin Investigator.
- If most owners say free in-product tools already answer the question, the idea is weaker than even this memo says: stop early.
