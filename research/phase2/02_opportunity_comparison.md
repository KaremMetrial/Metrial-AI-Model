## 1. Scoring framework (transparent)

Each criterion is scored 1–5 (5 = best for this founder). Weighted total is out of 100.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Why this weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Willingness-to-pay evidence** (any form, discounted when it is for detection/sync/recovery rather than explanation) | **20** | The Phase 2 central unknown; "problem exists" ≠ "will buy" |
| 2 | Dollar impact and measurability | 12 | ROI must be provable to renew; enables outcome-based pricing |
| 3 | Pain severity / urgency | 8 | |
| 4 | **Competitive openness** (5 = open; free in-product AI counts as competition) | **12** | Free Intuit/Xero/Shopify/NetSuite features erode price |
| 5 | **Data accessibility + integration ease** (Agent 5 colour mapped: GREEN 5, AMBER-GREEN 4, AMBER 3, AMBER-RED 2, RED 1) | **12** | Tiny strong-backend team, no domain staff |
| 6 | Time-to-value / onboarding burden | 8 | Human onboarding hours break SaaS economics |
| 7 | Accuracy tolerance (5 = forgiving) | 6 | Cent-level accuracy is expensive for a small team |
| 8 | Recurring usage | 6 | Retention |
| 9 | Expansion into broader Company Intelligence | 8 | The long-term vision |
| 10 | Inbound reachability | 8 | Founder preference; evidence in Agent 4 |

Weights sum to 100. Formula: Σ(weight × score) ÷ 5.

## 5. What the comparison says
1. **Willingness to pay concentrates where there is a counterparty who owes money** (freight, deductions, FBA, AP): contingency-priced, saturated, decaying. That is a services business, and a poor fit for a "Company Intelligence" first step.
2. **Openness concentrates where nobody has paying customers** (procurement, rep-override leakage, manufacturing variance): heavy integration, unproven demand.
3. **No category scores high on both** (Agent 3B), and none shows demand for *explanation*.
4. **Margin's expansion score (5) is real**: cost, price, mix, freight and discount data touch every other module. That is why it stays on the shortlist despite weak WTP.
5. **Cash/AR's data score (5) is real**: it is the only candidate where onboarding is hours, which is the only path to self-serve and inbound economics.
6. **A single Shopify + QuickBooks/Xero connection supports the top three on data feasibility** (cash/AR, margin, reconciliation), which makes one paid audit a clean multi-hypothesis test.
